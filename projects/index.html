    Projects¶

    Every project lives in its own subfolder of projects/ and is published at /apps/<name>/ next to its documentation.

    Project Live app What it shows
    ISS EVA Spacewalk Launch International Space Station spacewalks — real dimensions, orbit, Sun and EVA timelines
    CRS-35 Launch to Docking Launch Falcon 9 launch, booster landing, 51 h orbit phasing and Dragon docking for NASA's CRS-35
    Solar System Explorer Launch True-scale, real-time Solar System in the style of NASA's Eyes: real planet positions, time controls, flood or natural lighting
    AI Factory India Launch Illustrative 100 MW Vera Rubin NVL72 AI factory near Hyderabad, from the grid and dry coolers down to the Rubin GPU
    Brick Builder Launch Build with interlocking bricks in 3D: real dimensions, 34 parts, 24 colours, snapping, undo, replay, save
    Cloud Brick Architect Launch 2D AWS architecture building game: snap official AWS icons into VPCs and subnets, run traffic, fail an AZ
    CAD/CAM Studio Launch Parametric CSG part design, 2.5D milling toolpaths, material-removal simulation, G-code and STL export
    Forts of Swarajya Launch 14 forts of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj on real terrain, with a Sahyadri relief map and a 1630–1680 timeline
    Proto Lab Launch Electronics prototyping with 8 boards, breadboards and 65 sensors and modules: circuit check, simulation and Arduino/MicroPython/Python code
    Logistics Twin Launch Fulfilment-centre digital twin: inbound, putaway, AMR picking, packing, sortation and outbound trucks, with live KPIs, bottleneck detection and scenarios
    Robot Cell Studio Launch Modular industrial robot builder: kinematics, payload and joint loads, IK teaching, task programs in a work cell, URDF export
    Yatra · Rail & ST Launch Mumbai–Pune by Indian Railways (Vande Bharat, express, local, freight) through the Bhor Ghat, or by MSRTC ST bus up the Khandala Ghat: stations, signals, level crossings, monsoon, night
    Kumbh City · Nashik Launch Simhastha Kumbh Mela crowd and transport simulator: parking hubs, shuttles, ghats and Ramkund, the akhara procession, holding areas and the ICCC, with a costed plan and an end-of-day grade
    Turbofan Ops Launch Operate a high-bypass turbofan in a 3D cutaway: auto-start, TOGA, reverse, a live Brayton cycle, EICAS gauges and eight failure scenarios
    Brick Studio Pro Launch An advanced 3D brick builder: 69 parts with stud snapping, selection editing, a stability check, automatic instructions, generators, photo mosaics, parts lists and LDraw / BrickLink export
    Earthmovers Launch A 3D portfolio of eight heavy machines with working hydraulics: showroom, operating controls, configurator and quote, comparison, fleet planner, a digging sandbox and a job site
    PQC Road Works Launch Animated construction of a jointed plain concrete road as per IRC:15 / IRC:58 / MoRTH 600, from subgrade to opening, with joint drawings, a quantities calculator and a QC log
    Krishi Yantra · Farm tractors Launch Tractors with a working three-point linkage, PTO and drawbar and eleven animated implements, working a field and following a wheat season from stubble to mandi, with a field calculator
    Harit Ghar · Polyhouse & vertical farm Launch A naturally ventilated polyhouse and an LED vertical farm on a live climate model, run by IoT sensors and AI: model-predictive climate control, anomaly detection, simulated plant-health vision, forecasts and a 24-hour rules-vs-AI comparison
    Urja Maharashtra · State power grid Launch Maharashtra's grid from plant to plug: Mahagenco, private, central and renewable stations, Mahapareshan's 765/400/220 kV network and HVDC link, and Mahavitaran's consumers, with 15-minute merit-order dispatch, live line flows, N-1, frequency and six close-up sites
    Van Maharashtra · Forests Launch Maharashtra's forest cover from 2021 to 2050 as an illustrative pipeline of industrial projects clears and thins forest and breaks tiger corridors, under three scenarios, with mine, highway, dam and mangrove close-ups

    Shared conventions¶

    • No build step. Apps are plain ES modules. three is loaded from jsDelivr via an import map pinned to an exact version, so the folder can be served by any static host.
    • Real units. Scenes use metres (or the natural SI unit of the subject) so that published dimensions can be typed in directly.
    • Data separate from code. Specifications and scripted sequences live in src/data/, so they can be checked against sources without reading render code.
    • Modern UI. Glass panels, a transport bar, keyboard shortcuts, deep-linkable state (?mission=…&t=…&view=…), and responsive down to phone width.
