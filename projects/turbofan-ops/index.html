      Inside the engine

      Procedures

      Engine start: APU bleed on, ENG MODE IGN/START, ENG MASTER ON. The FADEC opens the starter valve and turns on an igniter at 16% N2. At 22% it opens the fuel valve, expecting light-off within 15 s. It closes the starter at 50% N2. Idle comes at about 58% N2. The FADEC aborts a hot, hung or failed start by itself and dry-cranks the engine. In manual start you must do that yourself.

      Take-off: advance the thrust lever to TOGA. N1 should reach its target in about 6–8 s with EGT below 1,060 °C. On a hot day the flat rating ends and the EGT limiter reduces N1.

      Landing: with the aircraft on the ground (landing roll), pull the lever below idle. The sleeve slides aft, the blocker doors close and the cascades throw the fan air forwards.

      Shutdown: idle for about 3 minutes to cool, then ENG MASTER OFF.

      Engine fire: thrust lever idle, ENG MASTER OFF, pull the fire handle, discharge agent 1. If the fire is still burning after 30 s, discharge agent 2.

      Flameout in flight: select IGN or continuous ignition. Ram air keeps the core windmilling, and the engine relights within the envelope.
