    Critical crowding alerts

      The Simhastha Kumbh Mela is held at Nashik and Trimbakeshwar every 12 years, when Jupiter is in Leo (Simha). The 2027 mela's Amrit Snan days are 2 August, 31 August and 11 September 2027 at Nashik.

      This simulator follows the city's three-tier plan: pilgrims leave vehicles at outer parking hubs or arrive by train, ride electric shuttles to transfer points, then walk through a vehicle-free zone to the ghats.

      Pilgrims move in groups of 250. Walking speed falls as streets get crowded (Weidmann's speed–density relation). Above 4 people/m² is treated as critical.

      On Amrit Snan days the three Vaishnav akharas (Nirmohi, Digambar and Nirvani) process from Tapovan and bathe at Ramkund at dawn, and the public bathes elsewhere until 09:30.

      The map is stylised and not to survey. Numbers (frontage, capacities, costs) are illustrative. The simulator is not affiliated with the Kumbh Mela Authority, the Government of Maharashtra or MSRTC.
