    3D Artifacts¶

    Interactive three.js visualisations of real engineering — built to published dimensions, driven by real physics, wrapped in a modern UI. Each project is a standalone web app that runs in the browser with no build step.

    Open the ISS EVA simulator Browse projects

    Projects¶

    ISS EVA Spacewalk¶

    The International Space Station at true scale (109 m × 73 m) in a real orbit with a real Sun. Play NASA and Roscosmos spacewalks: iROSA installation, battery swaps and Nauka outfitting.

    Launch app · Documentation

    CRS-35: Launch to Docking¶

    NASA's SpaceX CRS-35 in three acts: pre-dawn Falcon 9 launch and booster landing, 51 hours of orbit phasing, and Dragon's autonomous docking to Harmony.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Solar System Explorer¶

    A true-scale, real-time Solar System in the style of NASA's Eyes. Every planet is where it really is today. Fly between worlds and run time forwards or backwards.

    Launch app · Documentation

    AI Factory India¶

    A 100 MW Vera Rubin NVL72 AI factory near Hyderabad, from the 220 kV grid and the dry coolers down to an exploded compute tray, with the live Indian sun and climate.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Brick Builder¶

    Snap interlocking bricks onto a baseplate in 3D, in the real 8 mm stud system: bricks, plates, tiles, slopes and rounds in 24 colours, with undo, replay and export.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Cloud Brick Architect¶

    Build AWS architectures like bricks: six missions with live goal checks, budgets and stars, animated traffic, and an Availability Zone failure test.

    Launch app · Documentation

    CAD/CAM Studio¶

    Design a machined part from parametric features, then generate toolpaths, simulate the cut against the design and export G-code and STL.

    Launch app · Documentation

    गडकिल्ले · Forts of Swarajya¶

    Fourteen forts of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj on real terrain, from Shivneri and Raigad to Sindhudurg, with walls traced from the cliffs and a timeline of his life.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Proto Lab¶

    Prototype electronics in 3D: Arduino, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi boards, breadboards and 65 sensors and modules, with a circuit check, simulation and generated firmware.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Logistics Twin¶

    A live digital twin of a fulfilment centre: supplier trucks, reach trucks and racking, AMR order picking, packing, a sorter and line-haul trucks, with KPIs and bottleneck advice.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Robot Cell Studio¶

    Build an industrial robot joint by joint, check its reach, payload and motor loads, program it in a 3D factory cell and export URDF.

    Launch app · Documentation

    यात्रा · Yatra: Rail & ST¶

    Mumbai to Pune by Indian Railways through the Bhor Ghat tunnels and viaducts, or by MSRTC ST bus up the Khandala Ghat. Choose the vehicle, camera, weather and time of day.

    Launch app · Documentation

    कुंभनगरी · Kumbh City¶

    Plan Nashik for the Simhastha Kumbh Mela: parking hubs, shuttles, ghats, one-way routes, holding areas and the ICCC, then simulate 40 lakh pilgrims on an Amrit Snan day.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Turbofan Ops¶

    Start, run and reverse a high-bypass turbofan in a 3D cutaway: FADEC auto-start, a live thermodynamic cycle, cockpit gauges and failures from hot starts to engine fires.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Brick Studio Pro¶

    An advanced brick builder: 69 parts that snap by their studs, selection editing, a stability check, automatic building instructions, generators, photo mosaics and LDraw export.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Earthmovers¶

    A heavy-equipment portfolio: eight machines with working hydraulics. Operate, configure and price them, plan a fleet, dig in a sandbox and watch a job site at work.

    Launch app · Documentation

    PQC Road Works¶

    A concrete road built step by step as per IRC:15 and IRC:58: subgrade, GSB, DLC, slip-form paving with dowel and tie-bar inserters, saw-cut joints, curing, sealing and opening.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Krishi Yantra · Farm tractors¶

    Tractors with working linkages and PTO, and eleven animated implements: ploughs, harrow, rotavator, laser leveller, seed drill, sprayer, reaper, thresher and trolley, through a wheat season.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Harit Ghar · Polyhouse & vertical farm¶

    A polyhouse and a vertical farm on a live climate model, run by IoT sensors and AI: model-predictive control, fault detection, plant-health vision and a rules-vs-AI comparison.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Urja Maharashtra · State power grid¶

    Mahagenco's coal, Koyna hydro, solar and wind, Mahapareshan's 765/400 kV network and the HVDC link, and Mahavitaran's consumers, dispatched every 15 minutes with live flows.

    Launch app · Documentation

    Van Maharashtra · Forests¶

    Maharashtra's forest cover from 2021 to 2050 as mines, plants, roads, dams and ports take forest land, under three scenarios, with close-ups of a mine, a highway, a dam and a mangrove port.

    Launch app · Documentation

    More on the way¶

    Each new visualisation gets its own folder under projects/ and its own docs section. See Contributing for the template.

    How this site is organised¶

    Path What lives there
    projects/<name>/ A self-contained three.js app (index.html, styles.css, src/)
    docs/ This MkDocs Material site
    scripts/build-site.sh Builds the docs and copies every app to site/apps/<name>/
    .github/workflows/deploy.yml Builds on every push and publishes main to the gh-pages branch
