# यात्रा · Yatra: Rail & ST

3D journeys from Mumbai to Pune:

- **Indian Railways:**
  - Trains: Vande Bharat, a WAP-7 express with LHB coaches, a Mumbai local, or a WAG-9 freight.
  - The line climbs the Bhor Ghat through tunnels and viaducts.
  - On the way: stations, signals and level crossings.
- **MSRTC ST buses:**
  - Buses: Lalpari, Hirkani, Shivshahi, Shivneri or e-Shivai.
  - The road climbs the Khandala Ghat, past bus stands and oncoming traffic.
- **Driving:** an automatic driver follows speed limits, brakes for curves and stops at each station or stand.
- **Cameras:** chase, cab, side, window, line-side, aerial and free orbit.
- **Weather and time:** monsoon (with rain and waterfalls), winter or summer, at any time of day or night.
- **Sound:** optional horn and running sounds.
